**Montra — privacy policy**

_Last updated: <date>_

Montra is a personal finance tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. It is short because the app keeps almost everything on your device.

**What Montra stores, and where**

Accounts, transactions, budgets and savings goals are stored in a local database on your phone. Your
display name, chosen base currency, theme preference and profile photo are also stored on your phone.
None of this is uploaded to us: we do not operate a server, and the app has no account, login or sync.

The app disables Android's automatic backup, so this data is not copied to Google Drive by the
operating system.

Data leaves your device only when you ask it to:

- **Export transactions** writes a CSV file you choose where to send.
- **Backup database** writes a backup file to a location you pick.
- **Share** hands a file to another app you choose.

Deleting the app deletes this data. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never
received it.

**Advertising**

Montra is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, Google's SDK may collect and process
information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address,
and information about ads shown and interacted with. This is used to deliver and measure advertising
and to prevent fraud.

We do not receive your financial data, and we do not sell personal information.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app
shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads, and remembers your
choice. You can change or withdraw it at any time from **Profile → Privacy options**, which appears
when a consent decision applies to you.

Google's own policy applies to the data its SDK processes:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

**Children**

Montra is not directed at children under 13, and the app is not configured for child-directed
advertising.

**Permissions**

- **Internet** and **network state** — used only for ads.
- **Photo access** — only when you choose a profile picture, and only for the image you pick.

**Changes**

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be described in the app's
release notes.
